For Gulf institutions, one of the first SaaS questions is where the data will live. Ellucian gave part of the answer in February 2025, when it [announced Banner SaaS on the AWS node in the UAE](https://www.ellucian.com/newsroom/ellucian-banner-saas-now-available-aws-united-arab-emirates).

## What Ellucian announced

Ellucian's press release says institutions in the Middle East can use cloud capabilities "while being hosted in region, helping them maintain compliance with local data regulations."

Note the wording: "helping them" is not a statement that any institution is automatically compliant. The release also says Ellucian and AWS remain dedicated to expanding availability across the Middle East, but it does not name additional countries or dates.

There is a useful detail here. Kalba and AUD were announced as new Ellucian customers, not existing Banner sites moving from on-premises. If you run a long-established Banner system with years of local modifications, your project may look quite different. Ask for a reference that matches your starting point.

## What in-region hosting does not settle

Knowing the primary hosting location is a good start, but a SaaS service has many moving parts. Get clear answers on each of these:

| Area | Question to ask |
|---|---|
| **Production data** | Which AWS region holds our production database and file storage? |
| **Backups and disaster recovery** | Where are backups and recovery copies kept? Can they leave the country? |
| **Support access** | Can staff or contractors outside the country view our data? How is access logged and approved? |
| **Add-on services** | Are Insights, Ethos, Data Connect and Central Workspace hosted in the same region? |
| **AI features** | Where does AI processing happen, which providers are involved and is our data used for training? |
| **Subprocessors** | Who else handles our data, and how will we be told about changes? |
| **Non-production copies** | Where do test and training environments sit, and is their data masked? |
| **Exit** | In what format do we get our data back, and how quickly? |

> **Important:** This is a planning checklist, not legal advice. Rules can differ by country, emirate, free zone, regulator and type of data, so have your legal or compliance team confirm what applies to you.

## If you are elsewhere in the GCC

We did not find a public Ellucian statement confirming in-country Banner SaaS hosting in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait or Bahrain as of November 2026. That does not prove no option exists, only that we could not verify one publicly.

If your rules require data to stay in-country, raise that with Ellucian early. It could affect whether SaaS is workable for your institution, and on what timeline.

## What to do next

1. **Check your obligations first.** Ask your compliance team what must stay in-country, including backups and remote access.
2. **Put the table above to Ellucian.** Ask for written answers, and cover every product in your proposed scope.
3. **Map your integrations.** Regional systems such as government reporting, payment gateways and ID services may need rebuilding. Start with [what replaces direct database access](/blog/banner-saas-direct-database-access).
4. **Plan around your calendar.** Compare [real migration timelines](/blog/how-long-does-banner-saas-migration-take) against your admission and registration peaks.
