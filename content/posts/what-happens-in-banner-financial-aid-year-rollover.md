## Overview

The aid year rollover is **copy, then review, then test**. The copy is quick; the review is where the real work is.

## What usually rolls forward

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Aid year controls | Institution options and aid-year settings |
| Budgets | Cost-of-attendance components and budget groups |
| Funds | Fund definitions, award limits and disbursement rules |
| Packaging | Packaging groups and rules |
| Requirements tracking | Required documents and messages |
| Satisfactory academic progress | SAP rules and codes |

## What needs review every year

- **Regulatory updates** delivered by Ellucian for the new aid year.
- **Cost of attendance** amounts and budget components.
- **Fund allocations** and award limits.
- **Communications** such as letters and Self-Service messages.

## A practical rollover plan

1. Apply the year's regulatory releases in a test environment.
2. Roll forward the aid year setup in test and review every area above.
3. Load sample applicant records and run need analysis and packaging.
4. Compare results with expected awards for a sample of students.
5. Repeat in production with the same checklist.

> **Important:** Run a small packaging test in production before batch packaging the full population.
