## Overview

Banner knows **what a student has taken**. Degree Works knows **what a student needs**. Together they produce a degree audit.

## How the data flows

1. **Extract from Banner**: student curriculum, course history, in-progress registrations, transfer work and test scores are brought into Degree Works.
2. **Requirements in Scribe**: degree, major, minor and concentration rules are written as Scribe blocks.
3. **Audit**: Degree Works matches courses to requirements and produces a worksheet.
4. **Planning**: students and advisors build semester plans that can be checked against requirements.

## Keeping audits accurate

- **Curriculum data in Banner must be right**: wrong programme or catalogue term means wrong audit.
- **Scribe blocks must follow catalogue changes**: version requirements by catalogue year.
- **Course equivalencies and exceptions**: maintain them consistently.

| Audit problem | Usual source |
|---|---|
| Course not counting | Scribe rule or course attribute |
| Wrong requirements shown | Student's catalogue term or programme in Banner |
| Transfer course ignored | Transfer articulation or equivalency missing |
