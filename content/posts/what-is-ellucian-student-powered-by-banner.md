If you have heard "Ellucian Student" and wondered what happened to Banner, you are not alone. When analysts at the Tambellini Group [reviewed Ellucian Live 2026](https://www.thetambellinigroup.com/ellucian-live-2026-in-denver-a-conference-about-consolidation-pressure-and-position/), they found institutions still working out whether this was "a packaging evolution, a product transition, or a longer-term architectural shift."

This guide separates what Ellucian has said from what others have reported, as of November 2026.

## What Ellucian has announced

Ellucian [introduced Ellucian Student powered by Banner and Colleague](https://www.ellucian.com/newsroom/ellucian-redefines-traditional-sis-ai-enabled-innovation-and-unmatched-higher-education) at Ellucian Live in April 2025, calling it a new vision for its SaaS student information system. Its stated aim was to bring its integrated solutions "into one complete, unified SIS product."

From Ellucian's own material:

- **It is part of a suite.** Ellucian Student sits alongside Ellucian Finance and Ellucian Human Capital Management (HCM) in a SaaS ERP offering.
- **Banner or Colleague sits underneath.** Ellucian describes these products as "powered by Banner and Colleague."
- **Implementation is prescriptive.** Ellucian describes "a prescriptive implementation model" rather than an open-ended design.
- **The scope goes beyond the classic SIS.** The [product page](https://www.ellucian.com/products/ellucian-student) covers recruiting and admissions, student aid, curriculum, student success, lifelong learning, advancement and student management.
- **AI is built into the pitch.** The same page lists AI degree planning, an AI rule builder for curriculum and AI scholarship matching. Check which features sit in which package and whether they are live for your region.

## What this means for Banner's role

Tambellini's analysts describe the underlying objective as moving Banner and Colleague "into the role of a transactional and data backbone rather than the primary user-facing experience."

For a Banner team, that framing has practical consequences:

- **Banner data knowledge stays valuable.** PIDMs, term codes, FOAPALs and validation rules do not stop mattering because the front end changes.
- **Users may increasingly start elsewhere.** Dashboards, cards and AI assistants may become the daily entry point, which affects training and support.
- **Custom work moves outward.** Logic that lived in the database has to [move to supported tools and APIs](/blog/banner-saas-direct-database-access).

## Adoption so far

Ellucian reports growing momentum, though these are vendor figures and the groupings vary:

| Measure | Ellucian's figure | Source date |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS SIS and ERP go-lives in 2025 | 32 institutions | February 2026 |
| SaaS SIS and ERP go-lives in Q1 2026 | 26 institutions, its highest single quarter | April 2026 |
| Customers using at least one Ellucian SaaS solution | 80% | February 2026 |

That last figure counts any SaaS product, so it does not mean 80% of institutions run their core SIS in SaaS.

## Questions to ask Ellucian before you commit

1. Does our current Banner licence or Banner SaaS contract change? On what date, if any?
2. What is the support position for our current deployment, and is it written into our contract?
3. Which capabilities are in Essentials, in Signature, and priced separately?
4. Which AI features are live today, and where is their data processed?
5. Which of our modifications, reports and integrations does the prescriptive model not support?
6. What is the role of Ethos, Data Connect, Insights and Central Workspace in our scope?
7. Can we speak to a live institution of similar size, region and starting point?
